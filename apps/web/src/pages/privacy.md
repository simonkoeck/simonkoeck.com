## Overview

This website is operated by Simon Koeck. I take your privacy seriously. This page explains what data is collected and how it is used.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may collect technical data such as IP addresses in server logs for security and performance purposes. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

## Data Collection

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts. Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled to collect aggregate performance and usage metrics (page views, referrers, browser/device type). It does not use cookies, does not track individual users, and does not collect personal data. See [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) for details.

## Engagement Features

Writeup pages include a view counter and emoji reactions. Your IP address is used for rate limiting. To avoid counting repeat views, a shortened SHA-256 hash of your IP address is stored in Cloudflare KV for 24 hours. Aggregate view and reaction counts remain. Reaction preferences are stored locally in your browser via localStorage and are never sent to the server.

## Fonts

All fonts are self-hosted. No requests are made to external font services such as Google Fonts.

## Search

The search functionality runs entirely in your browser using a static index. No search queries are sent to any server.

## External Links

This site contains links to external websites (e.g., GitHub, NVD). These sites have their own privacy policies. I have no control over their content or data practices.

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, rectify, or delete personal data processed through this site. If you have questions or want to exercise your rights, contact me at [simon@koeck.dev](mailto:simon@koeck.dev).
